The dataset served by the MolMeDB API and its exports — including but not
limited to molecular structures, membranes, methods, interactions, proteins
and publication metadata — is dedicated to the public domain under the
**Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication (CC0 1.0)**.

This applies to the *data*. The MolMeDB *source code* (this repository) is
licensed separately under the MIT License — see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## What this means

You can copy, modify, distribute and use the data, even for commercial
purposes, all without asking permission.

## Citation

CC0 does not require attribution. As a matter of good scientific practice,
we kindly ask you to cite MolMeDB when you use the data, as described in
[CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff):

> Juračka J., Šrejber M., Melíková M., Bazgier V., Berka K.: MolMeDB:
> Molecules on Membranes Database. Database, Volume 2019, 2019, baz078,
> https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baz078

The data originally comes from the scientific publications and databases
referenced with each record; please also cite the primary sources you rely on.
